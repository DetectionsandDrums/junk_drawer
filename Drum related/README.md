# Drum Related

Odds and ends for drummers and drum-sample nerds. Right now this folder holds one
thing: a detailed reference workbook cataloging the sound packs (SDX libraries and
expansions) for Toontrack's Superior Drummer 3.

As with everything in this repo — take it, open it, and make it yours. Sort it,
filter it, add your own columns, or trim it to just the packs you own.

---

## `Superior_Drummer_3_sound_packs.xlsx`

A 27-tab reference workbook breaking down Superior Drummer 3 and its SDX sound
packs, instrument by instrument. Each pack's full kit list is transcribed from
Toontrack's product pages, so instead of clicking through 20-plus product listings
you can browse, compare, and search every pack's contents in one place.

### How the workbook is organized

It opens with two overview tabs, then gives every pack its own detailed tab.

- **Pack Index** — the map of the whole library. One row per pack, with the
  producer, the studio it was recorded in, notable FX / signal-chain details, a
  short description, and a status column. Start here to get the lay of the land or
  to compare packs at a high level.
- **Cymbal Genre Guide** — an advisory cheat-sheet pairing cymbal types (hats,
  ride, crash, thin/heavy crash, splash, china, stack, spock, bell) with the music
  styles they suit best. Handy when you're picking cymbals for a track rather than a
  whole kit.

### The per-pack tabs

There's one tab per pack — the core SD3 library, the numbered SDX packs (Area 33,
Custom & Vintage, Death & Darkness, Decades, The Drum Factory, Fields of Rock,
Hitmaker, Indiependent, Legacy of Rock, Metal Foundry, Metal Machinery, Orchestral
Percussion, Progressive Foundry, Rock Foundry, Rock Warehouse, Rooms of Hansa,
Stories, Modern Vintage, Stockholm, and more), plus a set of bonus "(extra)" tabs
(Drumopolis, Real to Reel, State of the Art, The Jazz Sessions, and an expanded Area
33).

Every pack tab uses the same four-column layout, so once you've read one you can
read them all:

| Column | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Category** | The instrument's role — Kick, Snare, Hi-Hat, Tom, Ride, Crash, etc. Section banners (e.g. "KICKS (12)" or a named kit) group the rows within a pack. |
| **Instrument (Size)** | The size or spec — e.g. `14x24"`, `5.5x14"`, `15"`. |
| **Brand / Model** | The specific drum or cymbal — make, model, era, finish. |
| **Best-Suited Music Style (advisory)** | A rough suggestion of where the piece fits — advisory only, not an official classification. |

### Ways to use it

- **Decide what to buy** — skim the Pack Index for producer, studio, and vibe, then
  open a pack's tab to see exactly which drums and cymbals you'd be getting.
- **Find a specific sound** — searching for a make or model (say a particular
  Ludwig or a Black Beauty snare) across tabs shows which packs include it.
- **Build kits by style** — use the Best-Suited-Style column and the Cymbal Genre
  Guide to assemble a kit that fits the track you're working on.
- **Catalog your library** — add an "Owned" column, mark what you have, and see
  the gaps.

### Making it your own

- **Sort and filter freely** — by category, size, brand, or style within any pack
  tab, or across the Pack Index.
- **Add columns** — an "Owned" checkbox, a price-paid field, a personal rating, or
  project notes on how you use each kit.
- **The advisory style column is a starting point, not gospel** — override it with
  your own genre tags however you hear the sounds.

Opens in Excel, Google Sheets, or LibreOffice Calc.
